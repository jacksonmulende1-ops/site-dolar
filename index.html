📈 How Compound Interest Works

Learn how your money can grow over time.

Understanding Compound Interest

Compound interest means earning interest on your original money and on the interest you have already earned.

For example, if you invest $100 and earn interest, your balance can grow faster over time because future interest is calculated on the growing balance.

The longer you keep your money invested and continue adding money, the greater the potential growth.

Always consider the interest rate, fees, inflation and investment risks before making financial decisions.

💳 How to Manage Debt

Learn practical ways to understand and manage debt.

Smart Ways to Manage Debt

1. List all your debts and their interest rates.

2. Make your payments on time to avoid unnecessary fees.

3. Focus on paying down high-interest debt first.

4. Avoid taking on new debt unless it is necessary and affordable.

5. Create a simple budget so you know how much you can safely use for debt payments.

Remember: good debt management starts with understanding what you owe and having a realistic repayment plan.
